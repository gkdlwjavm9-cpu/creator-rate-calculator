Questions

What is a business & b2b creator's sponsorship rate?

The niche multiplier is 1.70×, so a business & b2b creator with 100,000 average YouTube views charges about $4,505, and one with 50,000 about $2,255. On TikTok the same audience is worth around $1,360, on a newsletter about $3,615 per 50,000 opens.

Who advertises to a business & b2b audience?

Mostly SaaS tools, CRMs, hiring platforms, productivity apps. These are categories where a single customer is worth enough that paying a premium CPM still works — which is the real reason the multiplier is what it is, and the argument to use when a brand opens below the band.

My views are lower than the category average — should I use a lower multiplier?

No. The multiplier prices your audience, not your size; size is already in the view count. Discounting both is how creators end up at half their rate. If a smaller audience makes a brand hesitate, sell a bundle or a shorter deliverable instead of a lower CPM.

How often does the multiplier change?

Seasonally rather than annually: finance and B2B bid hardest in Q4 and January, travel in the booking windows, fitness in the first quarter. Hold the band and time the outreach rather than repricing the niche.
