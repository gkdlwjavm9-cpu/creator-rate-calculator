Who buys gaming on YouTube (long-form)

Game publishers, peripherals, energy drinks, vpns. Huge views, modest CPMs: the audience is young and buys fewer high-margin products. Volume and affiliate keys carry the revenue.

Format that carries this rate

A 60–90 second mid-roll integration inside an 8–15 minute video. Sponsors buy on the last 30 days of average views and ask for 60-day exclusivity in your category. A read that underperforms kills the renewal, not the rate.

Questions

What does a gaming YouTube (long-form) integration cost at 100k views?

$2,385 at the middle of the band ($1,620–$3,150), against $2,650 for a neutral niche — a 0.90× multiplier worth $-265 on one deal.

Why is gaming priced below average on YouTube (long-form)?

Because game publishers, peripherals, energy drinks, VPNs can afford to pay for a qualified audience here. The platform sets the format and the measurement; the niche sets what a customer is worth to the advertiser.

How should I quote this?

Send one number — $2,385 — with the average views figure and the period it covers, then name the deliverable and the rights you include. A range invites the buyer to pick the bottom of it.
