What does a education &amp; how-to YouTube Shorts integration cost at 100k views?

$715 at the middle of the band ($390–$1,040), against $550 for a neutral niche — a 1.30× multiplier worth $165 on one deal.

Why is education &amp; how-to priced above average on YouTube Shorts?

Because course platforms, language apps, certification providers can afford to pay for a qualified audience here. The platform sets the format and the measurement; the niche sets what a customer is worth to the advertiser.

How should I quote this?

Send one number — $715 — with the average views figure and the period it covers, then name the deliverable and the rights you include. A range invites the buyer to pick the bottom of it.
