How to use the answer

Quote the mid figure as one number. Keep the low end for a repeat buyer with a standing order and the high end for a brand that needs approval, exclusivity or a compressed timeline. The dedicated rate is not a negotiation lever — it is a different deliverable, with its own production cost.

If the monthly total looks unrealistic, the assumption to check is the deal count: most creators sell a third to a half of their slots, and the calculator lets you set that separately from the posting cadence.

Questions

How is the sponsorship rate calculated?

Average views ÷ 1,000 × the platform's sponsorship CPM band × the niche multiplier, rounded to the nearest $5 and held above the platform's minimum deal size. The dedicated rate is the top of that band multiplied by the platform's dedication factor.

Why is my number different from what a brand offered?

A brand's first offer is usually 30–50% below the band, because the band already includes the deals that settled at the top. It can also be lower if it includes exclusivity or paid usage rights, which are separate line items worth 20–50%.

Does the calculator store anything?

No. It runs entirely in your browser; the inputs are not sent anywhere and nothing is saved. The only thing that changes in the address bar is the query string so you can bookmark a scenario.
